Frequently Asked Questions

Why are your rates significantly lower than large Sauraha resorts? ▼

We are an authentic family-operated 15-room boutique retreat. We do not have high corporate marketing overheads, middleman travel agency fees, or overpriced buffet spreads. We pass all those savings directly to our guests.

What payment methods are supported? Is advance deposit necessary? ▼

Domestic and international guests can pay directly upon arrival at the retreat. We accept Cash (NPR, USD, EUR), eSewa, Khalti, and major QR mobile banking. Advance deposit is only requested for large groups during peak Dashain or New Year holidays.

How far is the retreat from Sauraha tourist bus stand? ▼

We are located approximately 1.5 km (a 4-minute auto-rickshaw ride) from the bus park. We provide complimentary pick-up service with our local electric rickshaw upon advance WhatsApp notification with your arrival time.

What are your check-in, check-out, and cancellation policies? ▼

Standard check-in is from 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM (flexible based on room availability). Cancellation is 100% free of charge up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled arrival.
