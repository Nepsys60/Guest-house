## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the quiet, mist-laden dawn of the Chitwan lowlands—marrying the raw serenity of riverside sal forests with the understated grace of traditional Tharu architecture. It balances tactile boutique luxury with an accessible, radical hospitality ethos (rooms NPR 500 – 1,500/night). The design aesthetic refuses ostentation, leaning instead into artisanal restraint, grounded natural textures, and effortless conversion architecture.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning eco-travelers, cultural explorers, solo backpackers, and experiential couples seeking sanctuary without institutional stiffness or inflated Westernized pricing.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Instant calm, rustic intimacy, unpretentious prestige, transparent trust, and frictionless booking clarity.

### Design Movement
- **Organic Editorial Elegance mixed with Utilitarian Conversion Architecture:** Minimalist layout foundations, refined editorial serif headlines, high-tactility warm ivory surfaces, and purposeful, high-contrast transactional affordances designed for decisive reservation flows.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the biosphere of Southern Nepal: deep jungle canopies, dried river grass, sun-baked clay, and weathered brass hardware.

### Functional Palette Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#3D4A3E` - Muted Sal Forest Moss):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, active date indicators, key narrative accents, and brand touchpoints.
- **Primary Dark (`#2D382E` - Deep Sal Canopy):** Dedicated to hover states on moss buttons, heavy footer zones, and immersive modal headers.
- **Secondary Accent (`#C49A45` - Muted Terai Ochre/Brass):** Used sparingly for conversion badges, key value highlights, ratings, and delicate focus indicators. Never applied across large backdrops to prevent simulated gaudiness.
- **Deep Neutral (`#1C1D1A` - Riverbed Charcoal):** The foundation for all high-contrast typography, high-impact icons, and crisp border boundaries.
- **Background Base (`#FBF9F5` - Warm Ivory):** The atmospheric canvas across all primary screens, softening eye fatigue while avoiding clinical starkness.
- **Surface Sand (`#EFECE6` - Tharu Loam):** Used across alternating card backgrounds, tabular lines, form input containers, and structural dividers.
- **Action Success / WhatsApp (`#2E6337`):** Direct conversational inquiry links utilize a calibrated deep forest emerald that honors both the jungle landscape and instant recognition.

## Typography

The pairing creates an immediate dialogue between tranquil editorial sanctuary and high-clarity travel utility.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for destination storytelling, suite nomenclature, evocative section intros, and quote framing. Sets a measured, leisurely pace.
- **Body & Numerical Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Drives all scanning tasks—dates, room amenities, instant NPR pricing figures, currency conversions, filters, and booking buttons. Maintains geometric structure, preventing readability bottlenecks on low-bandwidth or mobile devices.
- **Styling Directives:**
  - Category tags, room capacity, and availability tickers use `label-uppercase` with generous letter spacing to evoke hotel directory stationery.
  - Price figures are always anchored with `price-numeral` set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with optical tabular lining numbers, avoiding decorative serifs that could obscure low-cost accessibility (e.g., NPR 500/night vs. NPR 1,500/night).

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced 12-column grid anchors the desktop booking experience, transitioning gracefully into a focused single-column flow on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Scaling
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure. Margin: `1.25rem` (`20px`), Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`). Fluid full-width elements with dedicated bottom-safe-area padding for the persistent mobile booking trigger.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column structure. Margin: `2.5rem` (`40px`), Gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-max layout (1280px maximum content envelope). Margin: Auto centered, inner section horizontal padding: `4rem` (`64px`).

### Vertical Rhythm
Section blocks strictly follow generous atmospheric breathing space: `space-xl` * 2 (`5rem`) vertical gutters between narrative scenes, while functional transactional cards remain compact using `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) interior padding to keep booking parameters visible above the fold.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows, simulating depth through natural material stratification, subtle border lines, and delicate ambient light.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Earth / Canvas):** Flat `#FBF9F5` base plane. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Loam / Cards & Inputs):** Background `#EFECE6` or `#FFFFFF` bound by a hair-width structural border: `1px solid rgba(28, 29, 26, 0.08)`. Shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(28, 29, 26, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (River Stone / Floating Booking Bar & Flyouts):** Background `#FFFFFF` with high-defusion natural ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(45, 56, 46, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(28, 29, 26, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Canopy Mist / Modal Overlays & Drawers):** Backdrop filter: `blur(8px)` with tint `rgba(28, 29, 26, 0.45)`. Container elevation uses `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(28, 29, 26, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

With `roundedness: 2`, all core UI containers utilize balanced `0.5rem` (8px) geometry. This subtle curve softens the rustic architectural lines of the resort while preventing the playful casualness of fully pill-shaped surfaces.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem):** Buttons, inputs, search selects, rate badges, and interactive date tiles.
- **Containers (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Room cards, testimonial cards, amenity callouts, floating booking bars.
- **Modals & Drawers (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem):** Reservation modal dialogs and bottom sheets on mobile.
- **Pill Exception:** Exclusively reserved for small status badges (e.g., "Available", "Only 1 Room Left", "Verified Guest").

## Components

### 1. High-Conversion Floating Booking Bar
- **Desktop Placement:** Pinned horizontally at the bottom center or nestled within the hero boundary, shifting to fixed position upon scroll.
- **Anatomy:** Check-in/Check-out dates, guest counter, room category selector, and a high-contrast Primary button (`#3D4A3E` background, `#FBF9F5` text).
- **Affordance:** Clear, instant subtext declaring "From NPR 500/night · No Prepayment Required".

### 2. Room Rate Cards & Badges
- **Container:** Dual-tone card. Top 60% contains image frame with subtle 1px border; bottom 40% uses `#FFFFFF` or `#EFECE6` backdrop.
- **Pricing Badge:** Prominent `price-numeral` in Deep Charcoal (`#1C1D1A`) with currency denoted clearly (`NPR 500` to `NPR 1,500`). Includes a secondary brass/ochre tag (`#C49A45`) for transparent inclusions (e.g., "Organic Breakfast Included" or "Jungle View").

### 3. WhatsApp Direct-Inquiry Triggers
- **Visuals:** Compact button with `#2E6337` background, white label, and WhatsApp icon.
- **Placement:** Positioned as an immediate alternative alongside formal booking CTAs, recognizing local trekking and boutique safari traveler behavior in Nepal.
- **Copy:** "Instant WhatsApp Concierge · Local Tips & Direct Booking".

### 4. Tabbed Booking & Availability Modal
- **Layout:** Segmented tabs (1. Select Dates -> 2. Choose Safari / Experience -> 3. Guest Details).
- **States:** Active tab highlighted with `#3D4A3E` underline and bold text. Inactive tabs styled in muted charcoal at 50% opacity.
- **Forms:** Input fields set on `#FBF9F5` background, `1px solid rgba(28, 29, 26, 0.15)` border, transitioning to `#3D4A3E` ring on focus.

### 5. Tharu Storytelling Editorial Frames
- **Anatomy:** Asymmetrical image containers accented with delicate fine-line borders (`1px solid #C49A45` at 40% opacity).
- **Typography Integration:** Overlapping caption badges utilizing `Playfair Display` italic accents paired with `label-uppercase` geolocation markers ("Rapti Riverbank, Chitwan").

### 6. Sticky Concierge & Authentic Review Cards
- **Concierge Trigger:** Subtle round-cornered floating beacon on desktop bottom-right containing host portrait, name, and response time badge ("Hari replies in < 5 mins").
- **Review Cards:** Minimalist sand-tinted cards (`#EFECE6`) featuring verified guest badge, origin flag, date of stay, and authentic unvarnished quote in `body-md`.
